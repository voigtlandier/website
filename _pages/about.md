I am an engineering director at Google. I co-founded and currently lead the research and engineering team behind <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/project-starline-google-beam-update/">Google Beam</a> (formerly <a href="https://blog.google/technology/research/project-starline">Project Starline</a>). Google Beam is a new real-time communication and device platform that is changing the way we connect with people across distance.

Prior to Google, I was associate professor of computer science at the University of Virginia. I received a PhD in computer science from Princeton University and a BS in electrical engineering from Carnegie Mellon University.

My career has spanned academic research, applied research, and building organizations and products from the ground up.
